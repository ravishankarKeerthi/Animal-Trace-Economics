Cost allocation logic

Use direct issue where individual consumption is measured. For group feeding, allocate by a documented basis such as head count, body weight or ration plan.

Never assume equal consumption across calves, pregnant animals, lactating animals and dry animals.

Offspring economics

A calf should receive its own unique animal ID and a parent link to the dam and sire. Record birth costs, initial health, weight, ownership and future production or sale.

Breeding success is not just a pregnancy count. Measure cost per successful conception, calving interval, calf survival and offspring value.

Understand the calculation

Net cash contribution = recorded revenue − recorded cash costs. It is not the same as accounting profit.

Economic result can additionally consider current market value, depreciation, unpaid labour, shared overhead, financing and disposal costs.

Do not add current estimated value to realised revenue in the same cash-profit figure. Show it separately as an asset valuation.
